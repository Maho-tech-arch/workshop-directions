Directions / アクセス方法

Go up the stairs until you reach fresh air. You should now be standing in the middle of the campus, and you should be able to see our library.

Once you have located the library, walk past it with the library on your left. Continue straight until you reach a building with blue doors.

Enter through the doors and go straight ahead and up the first set of stairs. Then turn right. You should see another set of doors — do not walk through them, but turn right just before them.

You have reached your destination.
